# Your first reel script

Film this one first. It is short on purpose. The first time the engine runs, it downloads and sets up
the tools it edits with, so a short clip keeps that first wait small.

Every line below is also an instruction. When you say "this is a karaoke caption," the engine hears it
and makes that line a karaoke caption. So by the end you have one reel that shows you the whole effects
menu on your own face, with your own voice.

## How to film it

- Phone upright (9:16), face in the middle of the frame, some room above your head.
- Read it naturally. Flubs are fine: if you say a line twice, the engine keeps your last take.
- Say the lines close to how they are written. The words that name the effect ("hook," "karaoke,"
  "takeover," "count-up," "punch-in," "star," "thought bubble," "breaks away") are the ones the engine
  listens for. Everything else you can say your own way.
- About 40 seconds total.

## The script

1. This is where the hook goes.
2. Down here are single-word captions, one word at a time.
3. This line is a karaoke caption, so each word lights up as I say it.
4. And this is a full-screen takeover.
5. Here's a count-up to three hundred dollars.
6. This is a punch-in, a quick zoom for the line that matters.
7. Here's a star, popping on.
8. And here's a little thought bubble.
9. This one breaks away to a designed card.
10. Every one of these gets its own sound effect.
11. That's the whole menu. Now go make yours.

## Then say this to the engine

> edit this reel

When it asks how cooked you want it, pick **well-done** for this one. Karaoke, takeovers, the count-up
and the breakaway card are rendered effects, so they show up on medium and well-done, and well-done hands
you the finished video without opening CapCut. (Raw is still there for your real reels. It keeps things
simple and editable, and it is built to hold lighter styling.)

## Use this trick on every reel after

Any time you want a specific effect on a specific line, you can say it right in the footage: "here's a
count-up, five thousand dollars" or "this is a takeover." The engine builds that effect on that line. It
is the fastest way to get exactly what you pictured, and it keeps the build short because nothing has to
be guessed. You can also just tell it in the chat afterward. Both work.

Live effect previews run on GSAP, the engine's motion runtime. Footage, grade, and transition tiles use a stand-in scene. Sound and cover tiles are described, since they are audio and stills.
Motion and baked animation come standard on the rendered routes (medium, well-done, Animation). A color grade is optional: ask for one by name (cinematic, warm, cool, film, mono) and it goes on the footage only, never the graphics. On the editable CapCut route all of it stays yours to set in CapCut.

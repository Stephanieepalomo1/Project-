# Get your fonts & sounds (one-time setup)

Good news: you don't download or buy anything to get started. Your pack's special fonts live **inside
CapCut**: you add each one in CapCut once, and the engine uses it from there. (A few fonts are already
free/OFL and ship with the kit, so there's nothing to do for those.) The sound effects ship with the kit,
so there is nothing to add for sound.

> **How to add a CapCut font (one time):** open CapCut → add a Text → open the **Fonts** panel → **search
> the font name** below → tap it once. CapCut downloads it into your CapCut. That's it. Do this once per
> font in your pack and the engine will render your reels in it. Restart CapCut after adding fonts.
>
> **Sounds:** nothing to do. The reel SFX come with the kit (see Sounds below).

## Sounds (SFX)

The reel SFX ship with the kit: clicks, pops, taps, swooshes, dings, typing and more, already sorted by
the job each one does, so the engine picks a matching sound for every animation on its own. There is nothing
to search for or favorite. Say "library" to see and hear them all. (The public-domain ones, and where each
came from, are listed in `product/creative-vault/sfx/cc0/SOURCES.md`.)

Want the CapCut sounds you already love? Make a CapCut project that is just a shelf of them and say "learn
my favorites": the engine copies them in and reaches for them first (`product/favorites.py`).

Prefer your own files? Drop `.mp3`s into `product/creative-vault/sfx/` — the engine uses whatever's there
and **rotates** them so nothing repeats. Set your accent color in your style pack (defaults to white).

> the creator's personal reels use her own fonts (a hook font, a thought font, a takeover font) + butter
> accent — those are hers, not part of the kit. You get the same *system* with your pack.

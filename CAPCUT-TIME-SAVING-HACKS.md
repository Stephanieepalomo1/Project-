# CapCut Time-Saving Hacks (set these once, save them forever)

You only need these if you finish in CapCut (the editable-draft path). Do them one time and
every future reel gets faster. None of this is required to make a reel. It just removes the
little friction that eats a nap window.

Good news first: you set almost all of this up once, and CapCut remembers it.

---

## 1. Make CapCut open vertical every time (9:16)

Reels are tall. Set that once so you never fight a wide canvas again.

- When you start a project, set the aspect ratio to **9:16** (the tall one). It lives in the
  **Ratio** menu near the player.
- Already in a wide project? Change **Ratio → 9:16** and your footage re-fits to tall.
- If you always start from the engine's draft, it's already 9:16. This is for the times you
  start a blank CapCut project yourself.

**One-time move:** start a fresh project, set 9:16, and keep that project as your "template"
to duplicate. New reel = duplicate the template, swap the footage. No ratio step ever again.

---

## 2. Turn on the safe-zone guides so text never hides behind Instagram

Instagram lays its own buttons and caption over the bottom and right of your reel. If your
words sit there, they get covered. CapCut can show you exactly where that is.

- In the player area, open the **guides / grid overlay** (the little grid or "title-safe"
  toggle above or below the preview).
- Keep captions and key art **inside** the middle safe band: roughly the top is fine, but
  leave the **bottom ~15%** and the **right edge** clear for IG's buttons and caption.
- The engine already places animated captions in the safe zone. This guide is for anything
  you add or move by hand in CapCut.

**Rule of thumb:** if a word is near the bottom or the right rail, nudge it up or left.

---

## 3. Save your favorite text style as a preset (stop rebuilding fonts)

When you get a caption looking exactly right, save it. Then it's one click forever.

- Style a text layer the way you like it (font, size, color, shadow, outline).
- With that text selected, open the text style panel and look for **Save / Add to presets**
  (often a star or a "+" on the style row).
- Next time, select new text and click your saved preset. Instant on-brand caption.

Do this for your two or three go-to looks: your loud headline, your quiet caption, your
accent word. That covers almost every reel.

---

## 4. Favorite your go-to effects, stickers, and sounds

CapCut has a favorites star on nearly everything. Use it so you stop re-searching the same
five items every reel.

- Hover any **effect, transition, sticker, or sound** and click the **star / heart** to
  favorite it.
- They collect under a **Favorites** tab at the top of that panel.
- Build a tiny favorites shelf: your 1-2 transitions, your zoom effect, your click/whoosh
  sounds. That's your whole kit, one tab away.

Less scrolling is the whole win here. A short favorites list beats a big one.

---

## 5. Use export presets (set your reel export once)

Your export settings for a reel almost never change. Set them once and stop re-picking.

- In the **Export** dialog, set: **Resolution 1080p**, **Frame rate 30**, format **MP4**.
  That's the clean IG reel setting.
- Keep those as your default so every export matches. Name your exports simply so you can
  find the latest one fast.
- Only bump higher (60fps, 4k) if you truly need it. Bigger files upload slower for no real
  gain on a talking reel.

---

## 6. The hotkeys that actually save time (Mac)

You don't need all of them. These are the ones you'll use every session:

| Do this | Press |
|---|---|
| Play / pause | **Space** |
| Split the clip at the playhead | **Cmd + B** |
| Delete the selected clip | **Delete** |
| Undo / redo | **Cmd + Z** / **Cmd + Shift + Z** |
| Copy / paste a clip or layer | **Cmd + C** / **Cmd + V** |
| Select all | **Cmd + A** |
| Save the project | **Cmd + S** |
| Step one frame back / forward | **← / →** |
| Zoom the timeline in / out | **Cmd + = / Cmd + -** |

Learn **Space** and **Cmd + B** first. Play, find the bad spot, split, delete. That loop is
80% of hand-editing.

> Want the full list? CapCut has its own shortcut map in **Settings → Keyboard shortcuts**
> (you can even rebind them). The table above is just the high-value ones.

## 7. Main track magnet (ask Claude to turn it on)

This one saves a real headache, and it has a funny name. Main track magnet.

Here is the problem it fixes. Your main video clip sits on the bottom track. Your captions, stickers,
and sounds sit on tracks above it. Normally, if you trim a few seconds out of your main clip, those
extras do not move. They stay put. So now your caption lands late, your sound is on the wrong word, and
you are dragging every piece back by hand.

With main track magnet on, everything is stuck to your main clip like a magnet. You trim your footage,
and every caption, sticker, and sound slides along with it and stays perfectly lined up. You cut once,
everything else follows.

**How to turn it on:** you do not hunt for a setting. Before Claude builds your CapCut draft, just ask.
Say **"turn on main track magnet."** Claude magnets all your extra tracks to your main track when it
builds, so your trims ripple clean. Ask for it any time you plan to trim in CapCut.

---

## 8. Two habits that save the most time of all

- **Duplicate, don't rebuild.** Keep one finished reel as a template. Duplicate it, swap the
  footage and words. Your look, layout, and export settings come along for free.
- **Lock your words before you touch sound or motion.** If you re-cut after adding sound, you
  redo the sound. Words first, always. (This is the same rule the engine follows.)

---

### The one-time setup checklist

- [ ] Made a 9:16 "template" project to duplicate
- [ ] Turned on the safe-zone guides
- [ ] Saved my 2-3 favorite text styles as presets
- [ ] Favorited my go-to effects, stickers, and sounds
- [ ] Set my export default (1080p / 30 / MP4)
- [ ] Learned Space and Cmd + B
- [ ] Know the phrase "turn on main track magnet" before I trim in CapCut

Tick these once and CapCut stops slowing you down.

# Pick your 3 fonts (onboarding guardrails — the creator's voice)

> Shown to users building their own style pack. Voice = big sister across the table.

---

**You do not need to agonize over fonts every time you edit. Pick three, lock them in, and never open the
font menu again. Here is how I choose mine.**

Every pack is just **3 fonts, each with a job:**

**1. Your main font. The one that stops the scroll.**
This is your loud one, the first thing someone sees. It carries your headline and your full-screen
takeover moments. It should feel like you, but it still has to be readable when it is big. If it looks
amazing tiny but turns to mush at full screen, it is not your main.

**2. Your caption font. The quiet workhorse.**
This is your read-along captions, the words that follow along while you talk. Keep this one simple on
purpose. Clean and easy to read in half a second. This is not where you get cute. Its whole job is to get
out of the way so people just absorb what you are saying.

**3. Your accent font. The little bit of you.**
This is your handwritten, has-a-little-character font. It carries your thought bubbles, your side comments,
and your highlighted keywords. You use it small and you sprinkle it in. It is the wink, not the whole
sentence.

---

**The guardrails that keep it from looking messy:**

- **Only one font gets to be loud.** Your main. If two fonts are both fighting for attention, it reads
  busy and a little amateur. Let the main be the star and let the other two support it.
- **Pair opposites.** A font with character looks best next to a clean, simple one. Do not put two fancy
  fonts together. Big personality plus calm and clean is the combo that always works.
- **The half-second test.** If you cannot read your caption in half a second, it is not a caption font.
  Send it back.
- **Pick fonts that feel like you.** Soft, bold, editorial, playful. Your fonts are the first thing people
  feel before they read a single word, so choose the ones that match the vibe you want to give off.
- **Then lock them in.** Use the same three every single time. That consistency is your brand, it makes
  you instantly recognizable, and it means you get to skip this decision forever.

**Pick once. Then spend your time on your actual ideas, not the font menu.**
